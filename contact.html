Contact

Reaching out can be the hardest part.

For many people, making contact takes longer than deciding they need help. That’s understandable. You don’t need to know exactly what to say. You don’t need to have your thoughts organized. You don’t need to commit to anything beyond an initial conversation. If you’re considering therapy — or simply wondering whether it might be helpful — I’d be glad to hear from you.
